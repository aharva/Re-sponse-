# RE:SPONSE AI

### AI-Powered Customer Feedback Analysis & Automated Response System

RE:SPONSE AI is a full-stack AI-powered customer feedback management platform designed to help e-commerce businesses analyze customer reviews, identify critical complaints, generate personalized customer-support responses, and manage the complete response workflow from review analysis to email delivery.

The system combines **data analytics, rule-based complaint detection, Generative AI, human review, approval workflows, and email automation** into a single application.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Customer reviews contain valuable information about product quality, sizing, fabric, appearance, and overall customer satisfaction. However, manually analyzing thousands of reviews and responding to every complaint is time-consuming.

RE:SPONSE AI automates this process.
